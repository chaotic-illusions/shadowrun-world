Shadowrun 2nd Edition — Quick Reference

SR2 core rules · TN = target number · never below 2

Dice & Tests

Success Test: roll dice = rating vs TN; each die ≥ TN is a success.

Rule of One: 1s fail; all 1s = disaster. Rule of Six: TN > 6, re-roll 6s and add.

TN by difficulty: Simple 2 · Routine 3 · Average 4 · Challenging 5 · Difficult 6–7 · Strenuous 8 · Extreme 9 · Nearly impossible 10+.

Taking the time: base time ÷ successes. Defaulting: +2 TN per circle along the Skill Web (to a skill or an Attribute).

Karma Pool (refreshes each encounter), 1 pt each: re-roll all failed dice (each repeat on that test costs 1 more) · make all-1s a plain failure · add a die (max = base dice) · buy a success (needs a real success; the point is gone for good).

Combat Turn

  1. First turn only: everyone's pools refresh (not if surprised).
  2. Initiative = Reaction + Initiative dice. Injury modifier comes off Reaction first; Reaction 0 = no actions this turn.
  3. Act, highest total first. Acting character's pools refresh → declare → resolve → Initiative −10. Above 0 = acts again 10 phases later.
  4. Nobody left to act → new Combat Turn. Unused pool dice carry until your first action.

Same phase: declare lowest first, resolve highest first. Ties: higher adjusted Reaction, then natural Reaction, else simultaneous. Order: astral, then Matrix, then physical.

Delay: Free Action; step in at a later phase's declaration; carries over turns; using any pool dice breaks it.

Surprise: all roll Reaction vs 4 (waiting ambusher −2). Act only against those you out-succeed. Beat nobody and someone beat you: no actions at all for 10 phases.

Actions

Each phase: 1 Free + either 2 Simple or 1 Complex.

Free: call a shot · change smartgun mode · delay · drop prone · drop object · drop sustained spell · eject smartgun clip · gesture · observe · speak a word.

Simple: change gun mode · stand up / lie down (wounded: Willpower 2 to stand) · command spirit · fire SS/SA/BF · insert or remove clip · observe in detail · pick up / put down · quick draw (Reaction 4, +2 not holstered, then fire) · ready weapon · shift perception · take aim (−1 TN each, max ½ skill) · throw · use simple object.

Complex: astral projection · cast spell · melee / unarmed attack · fire full-auto · fire mounted / vehicle weapon · reload non-clip weapon · summon nature spirit · use complex object · use skill.

Move: walk Quickness m (+1 TN, rough ground +2). Run Quickness ×3 (dwarf, troll ×2), +4 TN to everything; run in one phase per turn.

Dice Pools

Refresh at the start of each of your actions. Combat = (Qui+Int+Wil)÷2; attack max = skill, resistance unlimited; heavy armor −1 per Ballistic over Quickness. Magic = Sorcery (+ power focus); spell max = Magic, Drain unlimited, never Conjuring. Hacking = Computer + Reaction (+ response increase). Control = Reaction (VCR).

Ranged Combat

Attacker

  1. Range to target → base TN (table below).
  2. Add situational modifiers (table).
  3. Roll skill + Combat Pool (max skill) vs TN. Count successes.

Defender

  1. Roll Body (+ dermal) + remaining Combat Pool vs Power − Ballistic armor.

Result

  1. Attacker ahead: +1 Damage Level per 2 net. Defender ahead: −1 per 2 net (below L = none). Tie = base damage. Defender's Combat Pool successes alone beat attacker's = clean miss.

Knockdown: Body vs ½ Power; need more successes than L1 / M2 / S3. None = prone; some = stagger 1 m. Deadly always knocks down. Called shot: Free Action, +4 TN, +1 Damage Level (SS/SA/BF only).

Fire Modes & Ammo

SS: one shot per phase. SA: Simple each, two per phase, 2nd at +1. BF: Simple, two bursts max, +3 recoil per burst; Power +3 and +1 Damage Level. A 2-round burst: Power +2, no level, +2 recoil. FA: Complex; declare rounds per target (min 3); +1 recoil per round fired that phase; Power +1 per round, +1 level per 3 rounds (max D). Walking fire wastes 1 round per meter between targets (not smartguns). Each burst/shot is its own test and its own Combat Pool allocation.

Gel: Power −2, Stun, Impact armor, knockdown TN = Power. Explosive: Power +1; all 1s = misfire hits the shooter. Flechette: +1 level vs unarmored; vs armor use the higher of 2×Impact or Ballistic.

Spellcasting (Complex Action)

Declare: split Magic Pool between the spell, Drain, and Spell Defense.

  1. Target in sight (own eyes, optics OK, no cameras). Base TN: living = Willpower (mana) / Body (physical). Objects (physical only): natural 3, low-tech 5, high-tech 8, highly processed 10+. Ranged damaging manipulation: 4.
  2. Modifiers: injury, cover, visibility; +2 per spell you sustain; +2 per stacked spell. Touch spells: injury only. Warded target: mana barrier +1 per 2 Force; circle or lodge +1 per 2 rating. Doesn't apply to spells from whoever made it.
  3. Cast: Force + Magic Pool (max Magic Rating). No successes = fizzle; all 1s = Drain TN +2.
  4. Resist (R spells): target rolls Body/Wil + Spell Defense (or own Magic Pool) vs Force. Damaging manipulation = Damage Resistance like a gun (armor, Combat Pool).
  5. Result: net successes. Tie = minimum effect. Combat spells: +1 Damage Level per 2 net; armor ignored.
  6. Drain: Willpower + Magic Pool vs Force ÷ 2 (down) + Drain modifier, no situation modifiers. −1 level per 2 successes. Stun, or Physical if Force > Magic.

Area: radius = Magic m, everyone in it; one roll vs each target. Withhold 2 dice: −1 m; 1 die: +1 m (max Force dice). Limited range (sense spells): Force vs 4, successes × Magic m. Sustaining: +2 TN to everything per spell, max = Sorcery. Multiple targets: split Force dice, one Drain test each. Noticing a cast: Int vs 2×(Magic−Force). Exclusive: effect at Force +2, Drain at real Force; fetish +1 reusable / +2 expendable.

Summoning & Spirits

Nature spirit (shaman): Complex Action, only in its domain, one at a time. Conjuring (+ totem, spirit focus) vs Force; successes = services; 0 = no spirit. Leaves at sunrise or sunset.

Elemental (mage): hermetic circle + conjuring library ≥ Force, materials 1,000¥ × Force, a source (fire, water, air, earth), Force hours. Conjuring (+ spirit focus) vs Force; successes = services. Bound max = Charisma.

Drain (always, even if nothing comes): Charisma (+ totem, focus) vs Force; −1 level per 2 successes. Magic Pool never helps conjuring.

  • Banishing (Complex, as exclusive): Conjuring (+ Charisma if you summoned it) vs Force; spirit rolls Force vs your Magic; per 2 net the loser drops 1 Force or 1 Magic.
