## Plot synopsis

Seattle has Super Brawl fever: the North American Urban Brawl Championship, the top-ranked **Seattle
Screamers** against the **New York Slashers**, live from a secret Barrens brawl zone in three days.
The runners' fixer sets a 10 p.m. meet in the back room of **Club Penumbra** with a Mr. Johnson who
really is one -- **Joseph "Baby Joe" Johnson**, the Screamers' manager. His two star players, the
heavy **Punch** and the outrider **Judy**, did not exist before 2050; he wants their skeletons dug up
before the game, 5,000 nuyen each, and no contact with the players. What he does not say: he bought
the pair's contract with Mafia money, bet borrowed yakuza money on his own team, and then learned the
**Seattle Mafia** had bet on the Slashers and told Punch and Judy to throw the game or swim in Puget
Sound. He wants blackmail material to force them to win.

Legwork leads to **Bubba**, a burned-out elven cyber-mage in a Puyallup Barrens walk-up, who has
just recognized Punch and Judy as **Top Cat and Starlight** -- his old teammates from the hottest
elven shadowrun team in the sprawl, who ran for **Aztechnology** until a 2048 datasteal against
**Gentech** showed them the corp was doing genetic experiments on live elves. They trashed the data
and quit; Aztech's assassin **Kyle Morgan** and his dragon **Perianwyr** killed the rest of the team,
and the survivors left Bubba to rot. Bubba has already phoned the Aztech fixer **Keane** out of
spite. Mid-sentence, Keane's mercenaries **Stark** and **Judas** kick the door in; Bubba throws a
manaball, goes invisible and vanishes into the squatter camps.

Then everything lands on the runners at once. Mafia enforcer **Carloni** and four soldiers arrive
at their doss with a fabricated story that they work for the yakuza and a warning to stay out of
urban brawl. Their fixer summons them back to Penumbra at 11 p.m.: Bubba is dead, a pro hit in the
squatter camp (Morgan and Perianwyr), and Keane has leaked that the runners did it, so Lone Star
wants them. Morgan tails them home with ten street hitters and asks politely what they know; the
attack is coming either way, until a Harley roars down the street with Judy rigging and Punch on an
assault rifle. Morgan escapes on the dragon's back. Punch and Judy warn the runners off, name Keane,
and ask them to remember the favor.

Keane receives the team in a dusty Bellevue warehouse behind a courteous doorman who is a Force 10
earth elemental, and appears only as an astral projection: a fixer who reveals nothing, but whose
legwork trail says he stole Slashers gear and uniforms and hired a decker to find the brawl zone. The
runners report to Baby Joe the night before the game and are paid. At 8 p.m. the next night, watching
the Super Brawl in a bar, they see Kyle Morgan's face in a Slashers helmet: his men are replacing the
Slashers one by one and will murder Punch and Judy on world-wide trid. Thirty minutes to sneak past
ISSV guards, officials and seventy-two camera drones into the Kingsgate brawl zone and fight beside
the two ex-runners. Win or lose, the game is voided, the runners' faces are on the ISSV's cameras,
Baby Joe is a dead man walking, and the team is on Aztechnology's list.

## Timeline

- **2012** -- Robert Howland (Bubba) born in Seattle. **2022** -- Kyle Morgan saves the newly
  Awakened dragon Perianwyr from Welsh farmers; the first French gang "Jeu de Guerre de Ville".
  **2037** -- first Super Brawl. **~2042** -- Morgan starts making hits for Aztechnology.
- **~2046** -- Franklin Hartman (Top Cat) arrives in Seattle at 33 and meets Starlight on an
  Aztechnology run; with Bubba and other elven runners they become the hottest team in the sprawl.
- **2048** -- the Gentech datasteal; the team trashes the data and quits; Morgan kills three
  (Slider, Rajah, Finder); Top Cat and Starlight flee to Boston, Bubba to the Barrens. **October 28,
  2048** -- Aztechnology absorbs Gentech Industries (handout).
- **2050** -- Punch and Judy debut with the Boston Massacre. **December 12, 2050** -- Dr. Carol Owens
  named Biogene's head of Biotechnology Research. **June 25, 2051** -- Yamatetsu buys Biogene.
- **2053-54 season** -- Baby Joe borrows from the Mafia to buy Punch and Judy's contract from the
  St. Louis Slaughter; the Screamers go from next-to-last to first.
- **Day 0, 10 p.m.** -- the Club Penumbra meet (game in three days; site announced 24 hours before).
  **Day 1** -- Bubba, Stark and Judas. **Day 2** -- Carloni; 11 p.m. the fixer: Bubba is dead;
  Morgan's ambush and the Harley rescue (Day 2 or 3). **Day 3** -- Keane; the second Baby Joe meet
  (night before the game). **Game night, 8 p.m.** -- the Super Brawl; second-quarter penalty, Morgan's
  face; the brawl zone. The book's own timetable puts all of this on "Day One".
- **Thursday June 4 / Wednesday June 10, 2054** -- the Update-Net: "Assassination Attempt During
  Super Brawl" (Punch and Judy resign from the league) or "Massacre at Super Brawl Game in Redmond"
  (Punch, Judy and eight Slashers dead; Joseph Johnson found dead Friday near the zone).

## Matrix systems -- to build in the Matrix designer (NOT built yet)

The book maps **no Matrix system** -- no node tables, no colors, no IC. A decker's whole job is
legwork (Etiquette (Matrix), 2D6-hour searches, one Shadowland request via an echo station found on
Etiquette (Matrix) (4), 8 dice, base time 36 hours) plus one abstracted intrusion. Systems worth
building if a GM wants them:

**1. ISSV brawl-zone camera control** (p.34). Controls the fixed, mounted cameras (four per block,
four per perimeter building); the drones' radio links are separate and can only be jammed locally.
The book resolves entry with the Virtual Realities Fast Resolution System at difficulty 4 (Average)
-- or, without VR, an opposed roll of 4 dice against the decker's Computer skill versus the decker's
highest MPCP rating against TN 4 -- followed by Computer (5) with three successes to jam the feeds. No
topology given; a Green or Orange host with a slave node for the cameras and a datastore for the
match recordings would fit.

**2. The ISSV computer system** (p.41). After the fight the runners' faces are on file here as well
as on world-wide trid; the book offers 'a shadowrun into the ISSV system' as one way to remove them.
Nothing more is described.

**3. Small fry.** Bubba's telecom (three call records; Computer (5), two successes, to identify
Keane's unlisted number) and his portable computer (an electronic hermetic library of his spells);
Baby Joe's pocket secretary and portable computer (Computer 2); Carloni's and Baby Joe's 100 Mp of
headware memory. The Gentech system Slider ran in 2048 and the Aztech system she checked afterwards
are backstory only.

## GM play notes

- An investigation that keeps turning into an ambush. The runners never control the clock: three
  days, the Adventure Timetable (p.9), and each encounter is a consequence of the last one. Keep a
  written record of **who told what to whom** -- Carloni, Morgan, Punch and Judy -- because the second
  Baby Joe meet (p.28) and his fate hinge on it. Telling the Mafia 'Baby Joe hired us' kills him and
  costs the balance of the fee.
- The Super Brawl odds are a barometer of what the street knows: 6-1 after Carloni, 8-1 once
  Aztechnology's interest leaks, 2-1 once its murderous intent leaks. Cap bets (5,000 on the
  Screamers at 1:1; 1,000 on the Slashers at 15:1) or the campaign economy breaks; a runner who
  borrows to bet is a subplot.
- Morgan's doss ambush is the real fight: ten wired hitters, five hidden, a dragon guarding astral
  space, and a Negotiation success that makes things worse. Morgan MUST escape (Perianwyr swoops if
  the runners corner him); Punch and Judy arrive after Combat Turn 2 on a Perception (4) and should
  read as heroes. Bend the rules so Morgan's men die or run rather than grinding it out.
- Keane is a scene without a win condition -- an astral projection behind a Force 10 elemental. Let
  the players learn from what he will not say; the payoff is the legwork they did to get there
  (stolen Slashers uniforms, a decker hunting the zone). Refuse violence: he leaves, and the GM adds
  whatever it takes.
- The finale is a 30-minute scramble: no shopping, no hiring, into the zone past guards (Stealth 4 x2),
  officials (Stealth 4 x3), drones (Athletics 5 x2) and mounted cameras, re-checked every two minutes.
  Choreograph it like an action movie -- only a few of Morgan's nine get a clean shot at a time -- and
  throw Screamers into the fight if it turns. The cameras are rolling: the runners' faces end up on
  world-wide trid and in the ISSV files, which is next month's job.
- Karma (p.41): 2 for telling the Mafia nothing, 2 for telling Morgan nothing, 2 for the truth about
  Punch and Judy, 5 for saving them, 3 for trying and failing, -3 for letting them die, 3 for Morgan's
  capture or death. Individual Karma per SRII p.199.
- Loose ends: Aztechnology's revenge (run it when the players have forgotten); Morgan and Perianwyr
  loose and off the corporate leash if they failed; Punch and Judy back in the shadows in Boston,
  owing the team; Keane as a future Johnson; Preacher's ork movement in Redmond; the Mafia and the
  yakuza both short a lot of nuyen with a dead manager to show for it.
