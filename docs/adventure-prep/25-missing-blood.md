## Plot synopsis

A simple affair. Fixer **Noggin** meets the runners at midday in **Ugly's** with a Renraku visitor's pass:
a nervous, pudgy UCAS sales vice-president, **Danial Simpson**, wants a present back from a girl who has
run off with it -- "no bucks, no data, no murder, just love gone sour". In the Renraku employee garage
(B3, Green 4B) Simpson, fresh from a back-slapping chat with his silver-maned father-in-law **Henry
Thomas**, hands over a photo of **Victoria Delling** (1414 Mayfair, Apartment 2W, Hollywood, Redmond)
and asks for **Blood**, a 60,000-nuyen ruby necklace by the jeweler Dante, back by 6:30 p.m. Friday --
1,000 nuyen each, 2,000 down. What he will not say unless pressed: Victoria was his mistress, his wife
**Lucile** has the receipt, he told her Blood was an anniversary present, and Friday is the anniversary.

Victoria's flat is empty and being mopped by landlord **Gary Lawson** (25 nuyen a question): a "friend"
with a signed letter broke her lease Monday and a moving van took everything. Across the hall **Mr.
Dent**, an old troll with eighteen cats, is holding her cat Nazgul and her life story -- murder-suicide
parents, institutions, a tarot reader called **Madame Ulishia** and "a policlub" that turned a
frightened child into a confident woman. Ulishia's Touristville storefront sells the same information
in 25-nuyen slices and points the team at a detective, **Patrick Bambra**, who was "following" her.
Bambra's Ravenna office (watched by five happy, brainwashed Brotherhood kids in an electric-red Westwind
with APDS in their Crusaders) holds a looped vid-message: Victoria, wearing Blood, telling Danial she
is joining the Inner Circle of the **Universal Brotherhood** -- a message Bambra lifted from Lucile's
phone tap and erased before Danial could hear it, because he fell in love with the woman on the screen.

Back at Ulishia's the door is open, the shop wrecked and two men with insect mandibles and shiny black
eyes are breaking the psychic's arm: Flesh Form Ant Warriors sent by another hive to erase what
"Ulishia" really was -- **Rektracir**, a Fly Spirit in a possessed host, raising the smothered
three-month-old **half-human, half-insect child** now lying in a hidden nursery. Bambra is found hiding
in the back office of **Superdad's**, a Kingsgate stripper bar run by his friends **Fernando** and
**Speedo**, just as four Brotherhood enforcers arrive to finish the beating they gave him at the
chapterhouse. He wants Victoria; the runners want the necklace; both are in the **Redmond
chapterhouse** at Belmont and Waveland, a converted four-plex cinema with a soup kitchen, a Force 6
astral barrier, an information-booth mage called **Judy**, a dozen guards with Flash-Paks, a Force 10
Fly Spirit sitting on fifteen boxes of loot in the basement coffers, and an Inner Circle Room where the
**Fly Queen** and four Flesh Form flies tend three cocoons. Victoria is in one, hairless and hunched,
as good as dead. Deck the chapterhouse Matrix and the Brotherhood's own records say why -- "Update
(8-23-51): Delling has already started generating interest. Proceed with Human muscle." -- and a
twenty-megapulse file called *The Universal Brotherhood* by the dead journalist **Zebediah Wanderly**
explains the rest.

Friday, 6:30, the **Glass Onion**: Simpson pays the balance, does not want to know where Victoria was,
and mentions that his father-in-law took him to "an organization that I think is really going to help
me" the night he hired the team. The bad guys win. Karma: the necklace 5, a copy of *The Universal
Brotherhood* 3, going into the Inner Circle with Bambra 2.

## Timeline

- **July 2049** -- Victoria Delling attends a Discovery Meeting (7-3-49) and joins the Brotherhood
  (7-12-49). **May 28 2051** -- Rektracir's host gives birth to the child. **May 10 2051** -- Lone Star
  closes the Grodin's Tavern fire (Wanderly and Icepick dead) as No Further Action. **June 2050** --
  the Wanderly/Davitt file flashes on Shadowland for three minutes before the server hub explodes.
- **Friday August 19** -- Bambra sees Simpson buy Blood at Crawford & Crawford; Toggle pulls the receipt.
  **Sunday August 21** -- Bambra gives Lucile the receipt; Victoria leaves her message and enters the
  Inner Circle (8-21-51); Bambra lifts and erases the message. **Monday August 22** -- the moving van;
  Bambra is beaten at the chapterhouse, stonewalled by Ulishia, finds the flat empty. **Tuesday August
  23** -- Lucile confronts Danial; Danial finds Victoria's phone dead; the Brotherhood orders "Human
  muscle" (8-23-51).
- **Wednesday August 24 (Day 1)** -- Noggin at Ugly's; 2:30 p.m. the garage; 1414 Mayfair; Madame
  Ulishia's; Bambra's office and the Westwind kids. Bambra has been dodging bullets for twelve hours
  and is in Superdad's back room.
- **Thursday August 25 (Day 2)** -- Family Feud (the Ant Warriors wreck Ulishia's); Superdad's and the
  alley crossfire; Battleplans; the chapterhouse lobby, the Frozen World, the Assault (business hours
  7 a.m. to midnight; the offices never close).
- **Friday August 26, 6:30 p.m.** -- the Glass Onion; the Simpsons' anniversary. Legwork: one contact
  per character every two hours, TN 4, running totals per section.

## Matrix systems -- to build in the Matrix designer (NOT built yet)

**Universal Brotherhood chapterhouse system** (Frozen World, pp.33-35). All four Seattle chapterhouses
run systems identical in layout; only the data differs (only The Octagon and Redmond hold anything on
Victoria Delling or Blood; every system carries the 20 Mp *The Universal Brotherhood* manuscript under
"Wanderly" or "Davitt"). Finding a chapterhouse's LTG: Computer Skill Test against the UCAS RTG security
code, TN 4, one success, after declaring which house. Directory number for Redmond: (63-7516). The
system's gimmick is **Orb**, a modified Access program: a small white sphere that detaches from the SAN
and follows the decker, chattering about his buried angers and inviting him to a Discovery Meeting. With
a talkative Orb most ice will not run unless the decker does something aggressive; a successful
Deception silences Orb and marks the decker as a logged Brother (Timothy just nods); Sleazing past Orb
means every IC fires the moment the decker enters a node. Members' passcodes give Orb-mediated access
levels. External Alert: two Major League deckers (SR1 p.116) enter as a metallic ant and a metallic fly.
Trace results send four green-shirts (Company Man) to the entry point within ten minutes. Worth building
as the campaign's signature "friendly" horror host.

## GM play notes

- Decision-tree module; build the horror slowly. Start it flat and simple (Noggin, the garage, a
  cheating exec) and let the strangeness arrive in the wrong order: happy gangers with APDS, a psychic
  who will not read cards, mandibles, a crib.
- The three-day clock is the engine. Legwork is one contact per character every two hours, TN 4,
  totals kept separately for 'Irishman on the Lam' and 'Behind the Brotherhood'; the team already
  starts with 6 successes on Bambra if they have his name. Turn table lines into scenes with the
  team's own contacts (the book suggests Ferret).
- Bambra is the moral pivot: he turns a 1,000-nuyen errand into a rescue and dies alone if the team
  will not follow him into the Inner Circle. Victoria cannot be saved -- do not let the players learn
  that until the sac is open. The bad guys win whether or not the necklace comes home.
- Rektracir is the Brotherhood's early-warning system: the first visit to Ulishia's puts the team on
  the hive's list, and the second visit should be timed to the Ant Warriors' raid. If a mage breaks
  the nursery ward early, run Family Feud immediately with four Warriors.
- Roughing up civilians is punished: Lawson's tenants, Superdad's fifteen bare-knuckle regulars, the
  cops two minutes away. 'Shadowrunners work in subtle ways and with a degree of aplomb.'
- The chapterhouse: visit politely and leave (Judy screens for magic; six guards arrive during her
  speech); deck it (Orb, Timothy, the orchard, the vault); or assault it -- Flash-Paks, twelve guards,
  a Force 10 fly in the coffers, the Queen below. Fifteen boxes of loot (1D6 x 10,000 each) keep the
  team low for a long time; Blood itself is unfenceable (TN 10, +4).
- Karma: necklace 5, a copy of *The Universal Brotherhood* 3, entering the Inner Circle with Bambra 2.
- Loose ends for later: Simpson (and Henry Thomas) inside the Brotherhood; the Ant hive in Everett
  that knows the shop was seen; two Major League deckers who have the team's entry point; the 25th
  Precinct memo; the Fort Lewis supply officer; a Brotherhood that 'should not be underestimated' and
  will, in time, come to burn the runners.
