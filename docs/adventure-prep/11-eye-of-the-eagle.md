## Plot synopsis

The Chinaman has 'a real looker, kinda scattered' waiting in the **Blue Flame Tavern's** thumbprint-
locked back room: **Farseer**, two meters of silver-white hair and golden skin in a feather-trimmed
coat, who is actually an eagle shapeshifter. A dying Haida named **David Tom** told her that a
'Haida' group in her home, the land called Tsimshian, has acquired 'a means to devastate the land, to
kill all living things that dwell on or above the earth'. She pays a fist-sized gold nugget (14,500
nuyen) and promises ten times that, and offers a pouch of owl feathers that summon her friend
**Tael**, a Great Form air spirit, once. Getting into Tsimshian is the runners' problem.

What is really happening: the Tsimshian government (Tsimshian and Tlingit tribes) has always ground
down the Haida and Kwakiutl. The **Haida National Front** turned to bombs, was shot to pieces by the
Peace Force, and went back to politics; its hotheads split off as the **Haida National Army of
Liberation** under **Daniel Sheemahant**. The northern arm of the CalFree eco-terrorists
**TerraFirst!** joined them, led by **Marta** -- a Toxic Dog shaman, an Avenger, who has Sheemahant
under Influence and Control Emotions spell locks and wants every human in the Tsimshian region dead.
Together they dug into a half-demolished US Army chem-bio lab under **Klawock** on Prince of Wales
Island and found **Red Masque**, a war virus that dissolves the mind over days, kills anything over
40 kg in twelve hours, and dies in sunlight within a day.

The trail: an HNAL missile takes out the Route 16 bridge in front of the team; the goons who fired
it were recruited at **The Randy Sasquatch** in Kemano's Haida flats by **Nightingale**; Kemano's
police roust Anglos and **Jawbone**, **Ghostwind**, **Zack** and **Dretch** deliver a warning in an
alley; the HNAL runs on a store-and-forward voice-mail box (LTG# 2604 (24-6051), 'Tyee Imports')
that cannot be decked because it talks to the phone through a speaker; the Tsimshian grid gives
up its address, **115 N. Fifth Avenue**, a derelict Technocracy hall where a wired filing cabinet,
a spell-locked appointment book, a Rat shaman, her hell hound **Poopsie** and a sniper wait. The
optical chip says **Wallace Blackwood** is at Klawock loading out the last of the Red Masque. Marta
sends **Cutup**, **Maumau** and the pyromancer twins **Smoke and Fire** to ground fireballs through
the team's astral sentry in their hotel.

Klawock: a rotting ferroconcrete base with Background Count 2, an elevator 30 meters down, a dud
FAE bomb in the corridor, ten vials of inoculant in a dead fridge, a hard drive full of Green Ring
and Project Red Masque, and Blackwood -- Owl shaman, Grade 4 Initiate -- with eleven soldiers and
four cylinders of virus. Across the Athabaskan border the newsnets are dismissing a 'Dead Zone' south
of Telegraph Creek; the **Polar Bears** snowmobile gang rode through Marta's field test and are dying
on their feet. The HNAL/TerraFirst! camp on the Stikine is 24 hours empty, but a dropped chip holds
the deck plans of the **TMV Queen of the North** and a chart reference in Douglas Channel, and a
scrap of Marta's blood on a door frame tracks her to **Hawkesbury Island**.

The ferry left Port Hardy when the chip was read and reaches the rendezvous ten hours later. Twenty
HNAL soldiers killed the crew and 35 passengers an hour out and are faking status reports; Marta,
Sheemahant, Blackwood and fifteen more come out in a gun-mounted Riverine and an Otter carrying three
oil-drum viral bombs on two-hour timers -- enough for a twenty-block dead zone -- to be winched
aboard, ringed with proximity mines and Force 4 wards, and sent into Kitimat's inner harbor on
autopilot. A rolling firefight with AVMs and assault cannon follows. Throwing the bombs overboard
or sinking the ship loses Farseer's reward; if the team fails outright, the **Skinwalkers**, a
Tsimshian shamanic secret society nobody will ever thank, have already neutralized the virus.

## Timeline

- **Before** -- HNF crushed ~5 years ago; Marta's northern TerraFirst! chapter formed ~5 years ago;
  Sheemahant met Marta 'over a year ago'; the Kemano substation strike (Prologue: 'a cold May
  night'; Legwork: 'several nights before Farseer approached the runners'); the HNAL bought the
  Technocracy hall 'several months ago'.
- **Seattle** -- the Blue Flame; Farseer; the gold nugget. Transport and Tsimshian entry papers are
  a mini-adventure of their own (no buses, twitchy airlines, slow boats).
- **Kemano** -- the Route 16 bridge (one missile); police in pairs, locals in packs; the Randy
  Sasquatch (Nightingale drinks for an hour, then hides for two days); Jawbone's alley; Hold the
  Phone (Kemano police at the decker's door ten minutes after a failed Trace); 115 N. Fifth Avenue
  (charge 15 seconds after the office door opens; police Citymaster ten minutes after the first
  shot). Wallace's voice-mail message is stamped seven hours before the team enters. TerraFirst!
  assassins hit the hotel the night the team makes a nuisance of itself.
- **Klawock** -- rotorcraft only; elevator up in 45 seconds; Blackwood's team is packing the last
  four cylinders when the team arrives.
- **Sunday Papers** -- 'several days after' Klawock: the Dead Zone newscast. Polar Bears: normal
  five days ago, all mammals dead three days ago, sick for three days. Camp abandoned 24 hours
  before the team arrives.
- **The chip** -- the Queen of the North sails from Port Hardy as the chip is read; rendezvous at
  F3-10.32.01/28.41.90 ten hours later; Kitimat twelve hours later; bomb timers two hours.
- **Handouts** -- Seattle News-Intelligencer, Saturday September 23, 2051: 'Yesterday's run' of the
  Port Hardy-Kitimat ferry was three hours late (success) or crashed into a Kitimat service dock
  on a faulty autopilot (failure). Farseer pays the balance at the Blue Flame.

## Matrix systems -- to build in the Matrix designer (NOT built yet)

The book maps no node tables. Three systems are described in enough detail to build if wanted:

**Tsimshian regional telecom grid (RTG/LTG lookup)** -- 'Decking into the Tsimshian LTG to dig up the
physical location of LTG# 2604 (24-6051) is easy'; handled in the abstract (SAN straight to the
datastore) or with the Virtual Realities quick system. Suggested minimal build:

| Node | Function | Rating / IC |
|---|---|---|
| SAN | Public entry to the Tsimshian grid | Orange-5 |
| DS-1 | Subscriber directory: LTG numbers to street addresses (115 N. Fifth Avenue) | Orange-5; Gray Trace and Report -- three successes vs TN 5 to download; a failed fight with the ice puts eight Kemano police at the decker's door in ten minutes |

**HNAL voice-mail system ('Tyee Imports', LTG# 2604 (24-6051))** -- not a host. A vid phone with the
video disabled and a small PC coupled by speaker and microphone: the phone opens in hands-free mode
and the machine listens, so there is no electronic connection to deck. Computer TN 4 to realize it is
impossible (zero successes: the decker thinks the security is just better than he is). Features:
voice-recognition mailbox identifiers (officers' first names -- Daniel, Wallace, Marta), single-word
retrieval passwords (Jawbone 'Freedom', Ghostwind 'Frag you'), star to send a fax, encrypted
messages on an optical chip (Computer TN 8 to decrypt once the chip is in hand). Slightly degraded
sound quality is the clue.

**Klawock weapons lab minicomputer** -- four terminals, decades-dead microprocessor; nothing to
deck. Pull the magnetic hard drive (Computer TN 5 to identify, Computer B/R TN 4 to jury-rig), no IC,
simple encryption. Contents on the Klawock Weapons Lab row (Green Ring 1-3, Project Red Masque,
the vaccine formula).

**Other electronics** -- the viral bomb timers (TN 11 with the right interface; anti-tamper slags
the circuitry on a failure), the Queen of the North's autopilot (Pilot 2; reprogrammable, with an
environmental catastrophe attached), and public databases (Klawock, the ferry schedule, chart
reference F3-10.32.01/28.41.90 at Computer TN 6 if nobody has Navigation, Motorboat or Sailboat).

## GM play notes

- Farseer is not a Mr. Johnson: play the cocked head and darting eyes, the gaps in her knowledge of
  governments and terrorism, and the anger if the virus goes into the sea. Ask the players where
  David Tom died; if nobody does, let her tell them what boneheads they are.
- Getting into Tsimshian is a mini-adventure (papers, wheels, the border). Then keep the rain on.
- Kemano is a tightrope: information flows both ways, the police are looking for an excuse, and
  the Randy Sasquatch punishes both rudeness and wimpiness. Every clue has a backup (matches,
  scraps of paper, the grapevine); use the voice-mail number as the short-cut if the tavern's
  significance is lost.
- 115 N. Fifth Avenue is a three-layer trap (hell hound on astral scouts, the wired cabinet, the
  spell-locked book) with a gauntlet outside and police in ten minutes. Let the sniper pick the
  obvious magician.
- Smoke and Fire ground fire spells through the astral sentry into the hotel room. Warn nobody.
- Klawock: make them fear the air (it is clean), the FAE bomb (a chance shot cannot set it all off)
  and the identical chemsuits (which one is Blackwood?). The hard drive is the world's most
  dangerous paydata; MCT is the wrong buyer.
- The Dead Zone is safe and the Polar Bears are a nuisance, not a killer; fudge to keep the team up.
  The camp gives a clock: ten hours to the rendezvous.
- The ferry is the set piece: heavy weapons, AVMs, an assault cannon, mines, wards and timers, and
  35 dead passengers below decks. Dumping the bombs is an environmental catastrophe and forfeits
  the reward; taking the ship early only postpones Marta. Tael is the one-time out.
- Aftermath: the Tsimshian government admits nothing; Marta at large is a campaign villain with a
  Threat Rating that grows; the Skinwalkers are the secret that lets a failed team survive and the
  hook for finding out who really saved Kitimat.
